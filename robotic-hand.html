Robotic Hand

A CAD model of a 5-finger robotic hand created with multiple subassemblies and inspired from real, human hands (to improve grasp).

Human-inspired Design

For this project, I focused on incorporating functional features inspired by the human hand. I integrated fingernails into the robotic hand design to improve manipulation of small objects and explored CAD techniques, including loft guide rails, to create complex organic geometries.

Testing & Iteration

To improve dexterity, the fingers were designed with two degrees of freedom at the base joint. While inspired by the human hand, this configuration provides greater mobility than the comparatively restricted motion available in human finger base joints.
