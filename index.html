About Me

I'm an Engineering Physics major at the University of British Columbia with a strong interest in hardware design and problem-solving. As part of the Arm subteam on the UBC Rover Design Team, I used CAD and ECAD tools like SolidWorks and Altium Designer to improve the rover's arm. My experience spans robotics, programming (Java, C, and C++), and mechanical design, supported by a solid foundation in math and physics. I enjoy taking things apart to understand how they work — and then figuring out how to make them work better.
