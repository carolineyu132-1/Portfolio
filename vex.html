Throughout my five years with VEX Robotics, I've developed strong skills in mechanical design, autonomous programming, sensor integration, and engineering notebooking. Beyond the technical side, leading a team through high-pressure situations and difficult decisions has taught me invaluable soft skills in communication, collaboration, and leadership.

Ballistic Mechanisms

In the VEX Over Under competition, our team built a catapult to launch a Triball over half the arena, using rubber bands for power and a slip gear to control timing and release. Designing it taught me to balance theoretical physics with real-world constraints (calculating launch angles, gear ratios, and motor capability) and gave me a much stronger grasp of torque, energy transfer, and iterative design, along with the problem-solving skills to troubleshoot when the mechanism didn't perform as predicted.

Intake and Pneumatic Systems

In the VEX High Stakes competition, our robot was designed to intake rings and place them accurately on both wall and mobile stakes. Through this build, I deepened my understanding of reliable intake mechanisms and precise placement systems, refining each design through repeated testing and iteration. I also gained hands-on experience with pneumatics and linear motion, learning how these systems functioned and how to fine-tune them for consistent, reliable performance under competition conditions.
